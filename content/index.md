## Frequently asked questions

### Do you offer online therapy across India and for clients abroad?

Yes. All sessions are online via secure video or audio calls for clients in Delhi, Gurugram, across India, and abroad, by appointment only.

### How much does a therapy session cost?

One session is ₹1,500. A 3-session package is ₹1,400 per session (₹4,200 total). A 6-session package is ₹1,200 per session (₹7,200 total). Student discounts are available — message us on WhatsApp with a valid student ID to ask about the current rate.

### How much does online therapy cost if I live outside India?

Online sessions for clients outside India are US$35 per session, on video or audio, in English or Hindi. Message us on WhatsApp to book, and we'll share payment details and help you find a time that works in your time zone.

### What happens in a therapy session?

Sessions are a confidential space to talk through what's on your mind. Dr. Jindal will listen, help you identify patterns, and work with you to develop practical coping strategies and insights. Each 50-minute session is tailored to your specific concerns.

### How do I prepare for my first session?

No special preparation needed. Find a quiet, private space where you feel comfortable. Think about what brings you to therapy, but you don't need to have everything organised — we'll explore together.

### Is my information confidential?

Yes, completely. All sessions are private and confidential, following professional ethical guidelines for psychologists in India. Information is only shared with your explicit consent or in rare legally required circumstances (such as risk of harm).

### Do you offer free therapy?

Reach for Peace is a paid professional service. If you need free support, please contact Tele-MANAS at 14416 — a 24×7 government mental health helpline available across India.

### Is Reach for Peace a 24×7 service?

No, we operate by appointment only, Monday to Sunday. This is not an emergency or crisis service. For 24×7 crisis support, please contact Tele-MANAS at 14416.

### What concerns can you help with?

We support you navigating anxiety, stress, burnout, overthinking, relationship challenges, family dynamics, low mood, and life transitions. If you're unsure whether therapy is right for your situation, book a fit call to discuss.

### Who is the therapist?

Dr. Lisha Jindal is a psychologist and founder of RFP, with an M.A. & Psy.D (HC) in clinical psychology, along with specialised training in relationship & family therapy & Life coaching. She brings a warm, individualised approach to counselling for anxiety, stress, relationship challenges, attachment issues, overthinking and life transitions.

### What languages do you offer therapy in?

Sessions are available in English and Hindi.

### How do I book a session?

Send a message via WhatsApp or email with a brief note about what brings you here. We'll arrange a 15-minute fit call, and if it's a good match, we'll schedule your first paid session.

### Can you prescribe medication?

No. As a psychologist, Dr. Jindal provides therapy and counselling but does not prescribe medication. If medication is needed, we can coordinate with a psychiatrist.

### Do you work with NRIs or clients in different time zones?

Case-by-case basis. Sessions are scheduled during Indian Standard Time (IST) working hours, Monday to Sunday. Please inquire if you're in a different time zone.
