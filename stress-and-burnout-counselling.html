How do I know if I’m experiencing burnout?

Burnout can build gradually when prolonged stress leaves you feeling physically and emotionally drained, whether from work, studies, family responsibilities, or caregiving.

Exhausted all the time

Tired even after a weekend or a holiday, as if no amount of sleep is enough.

Can’t switch off

Checking work messages late at night, or thinking about deadlines and targets even when you’re with family.

Short fuse, low patience

Snapping at people you love, or feeling irritated by small things that never used to bother you.

Losing interest and drive

Work or studies you once cared about now feel pointless, and getting through the day feels like a grind.

What does therapy for stress & burnout involve?

Therapy starts with understanding what’s been wearing you down and how it’s showing up in your days, your mood and your relationships. Together, you’ll find ways to cope that actually fit your life, notice what keeps you stuck, and learn to say no without guilt.

There’s no one-size-fits-all plan. Sessions are shaped around you and what you’re going through.

Map what’s draining you

Look at where your energy goes each week, and which pressures are within your control and which aren’t.

Learn to recover, not just push through

Practical ways to rest, unwind and recharge that fit into a busy Indian workday or exam season.

Set limits that feel okay

Practise saying no, asking for help and handling expectations from your boss, teachers or family without guilt.

Reconnect with what matters

Rebuild a pace of life you can sustain, with room for the people and things you care about.

Sessions are non-judgemental. You set the pace, and nothing is forced.

Common questions about therapy for stress & burnout

How do I know if I’m stressed or burned out?

Stress can feel like you’re overwhelmed by too many demands, while burnout can leave you feeling emotionally and physically drained, detached, or unable to recover even after resting. The two can overlap, but if exhaustion and detachment persist for a while, it may be a sign that you’re experiencing burnout rather than short-term stress.

Can therapy help with work stress or burnout?

Yes. Therapy can help you understand what’s contributing to your stress or burnout and develop practical ways to cope, set healthier limits, and recover. The approach is evidence-based and adapted to your circumstances, as what helps can vary from person to person.

Do I need to quit my job or take a break to get better?

Not necessarily. Some people work or study while addressing burnout, while others may need time away to recover. Therapy can help you understand what you need, explore your options, and make decisions about work or studies with greater clarity rather than from a place of overwhelm.

I’m a student under exam pressure. Is this for me?

Yes. Exam pressure, competitive entrance tests, college deadlines and family expectations can all lead to stress and burnout. A student discount is available; message us on WhatsApp with a valid student ID to ask about the current rate.

Are sessions online, and can I take them in Hindi?

Yes. Sessions are conducted online by secure video or audio, by appointment, and are available in both English and Hindi. You can join from any quiet, private space that works for you.

How do I know if therapy is right for me?

You don’t need to be at a breaking point to seek therapy. If stress is affecting your energy, mood, work, studies, relationships, or ability to switch off, therapy can be a useful space to understand what’s happening and work on it.
