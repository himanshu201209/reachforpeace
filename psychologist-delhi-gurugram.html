Common questions from Delhi and Gurugram

Where are you based?

Dr. Lisha Jindal is based in Gurugram, Delhi NCR. Sessions happen by secure video or audio, so you can join from home, work, or anywhere you have a little privacy.

Which areas do you work with?

People join from all over Delhi and Gurugram (Gurgaon), other parts of India, and abroad.

What times are sessions available?

Monday to Sunday, by appointment. Send a WhatsApp message and we’ll find a time that fits around your work and commute.

Can I have sessions in Hindi?

Yes. Sessions are in English or Hindi, whichever feels easier for you.

What do I need for a session?

A phone or laptop, a steady internet connection and a quiet spot. Headphones help. Each session is 50 minutes, by video or audio.

One session is ₹1,500. A 3-session package is ₹1,400 per session (₹4,200 total), and a 6-session package is ₹1,200 per session (₹7,200 total). Couples sessions are ₹3,500 per session.
