How does career counselling work?

Career counselling is a full program of 3 to 4 sessions. Each step builds on the last, so by the end you have a clear picture and a plan you can actually use.

Getting to know you

You talk about where you are now, what you’ve tried and what’s on your mind.

Simple assessments

Short tests that show how you think, what you’re naturally good at, what you’re like as a person and what you enjoy.

Guidance made for you

The career counsellor goes through your results with you and talks about options that suit you.

Your career report

In the final session, you get a detailed career report to keep and come back to.

Not looking for career help? You can also book regular sessions to talk through life goals, a big decision or what you want next.

Common questions about career counselling

Who is career counselling for?

It’s for students choosing a stream or course, graduates working out what’s next, and people thinking about a career change. If you feel unsure about your direction, it can help.

What assessments are included?

There are four: intelligence, aptitude, personality and interest. Put simply, they look at how you think, what you’re good at, what you’re like and what you enjoy. They aren’t exams, so there’s no pass or fail.

How many sessions does it take?

The program runs over 3 to 4 sessions. You pay one fee for the full program, so you know the cost from the start.

What’s in the career report?

It brings together what came out of your assessments and sessions, so it’s all in one place. You get it in the final session, to keep and come back to.

Can I do this online and in Hindi?

Yes. All sessions are online, by secure video or audio, by appointment. You can have them in English or Hindi.

Is it only about careers, or life direction too?

Both. If you’re stuck on life goals or a big decision rather than a career, you can book regular sessions instead, starting at ₹1,500 for 50 minutes.
